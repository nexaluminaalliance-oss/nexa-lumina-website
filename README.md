# Nexa Lumina Alliance Website

Customer-facing product catalogue and sales storefront for Nexa Lumina Alliance.

## Purpose

The website presents solar, energy-storage, portable-power and related products that NLA can sell. Its primary commercial path is:

**Product discovery → product enquiry → WhatsApp/contact → availability & price confirmation → sale**

The site is **not** the Supplier Intelligence system and is not a public procurement database. Supplier research, manufacturer verification, wholesale economics and reseller-channel intelligence remain behind the storefront.

## Product source

The initial product universe was derived from the supplied **ZX Catalogue official.pdf**. Prices are intentionally omitted. The catalogue is used to identify product families and candidate products, not to establish NLA pricing, manufacturer authorization, warranty terms, current stock or Kenya distribution rights.

Before a product is published with a price, NLA should verify the relevant official manufacturer/authorized-channel information and determine its own landed cost and selling price.

See `catalogue.md` for the initial product universe.

## Current state

This is an initial static, responsive storefront implementation. Product imagery, verified brands/models, current availability, official-channel evidence, NLA selling prices and final public contact details remain to be populated as they are verified.

## Commercial principle

NLA should not become a copy of a supplier catalogue or publish unverified supplier prices. The public site should show products NLA can actually offer, with clear specifications and a direct path to enquiry and sale.

## Before production launch

1. Replace placeholders with verified product imagery and model information.
2. Verify manufacturer/authorized-channel relationships where claimed.
3. Confirm current availability and NLA landed economics.
4. Add NLA selling prices when commercially approved.
5. Verify WhatsApp and other public contact details.
6. Add legal/privacy pages and domain configuration.
7. Test all product and enquiry links on mobile and desktop.
