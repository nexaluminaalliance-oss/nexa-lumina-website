## Commercial rule

This catalogue is a **product-universe source**, not a pricing source. NLA will not publish the ZX catalogue prices. Before a product is publicly priced, verify current manufacturer/authorized-channel economics and NLA's actual landed cost, then set the selling price separately.

## Source integrity

Product names, capacities and category coverage above are extracted from the supplied ZX catalogue. Manufacturer authorization, current stock, official wholesale pricing, warranty terms and Kenya distribution rights are **not established by this catalogue alone**.
